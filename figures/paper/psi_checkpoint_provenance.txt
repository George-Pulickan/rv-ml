the CSV above reproduces these files.

Checkpoints that must NOT be used
---------------------------------
checkpoints/regression_mlp_74.pt (present on the laptop-sync branch, renamed
there on 2026-07-27 to make the hazard obvious) is a 15-epoch smoke artifact
that predicts near the training mean. It is not on main. Passing --checkpoint
explicitly, as the sbatch script does, avoids it.

Superseded pins
---------------
Two earlier files exist outside the repository and describe DIFFERENT trainings:
regression_mlp_74_paper_20260726-0946.pt (sha256 4c09059a...) and
regression_mlp_74_paper.pt (sha256 30f40259...), both from 2026-07-26. An
earlier version of this note documented the first of those as the paper
checkpoint. That was wrong: no reported number came from it. The 2026-07-27
seed sweep above supersedes both.
